Info

Our development team is working on ways to help ensure that A Township Tale continues to thrive after Alta's servers are shut down. We will share updates on our progress as soon as we have something ready to showcase.

Current Focus

  • PC support is our primary focus at this time.
  • Quest support is something we would love to make possible, but we cannot make any guarantees.
  • We are also exploring ways to support the future growth of the game through modding and community-driven development. At this stage, we are still evaluating what that may look like.

Addressing a Common Concern

We understand that some members of our development team have had a controversial history within the community. These individuals are involved because of their knowledge and expertise, which can help us achieve our goal of keeping the game safe, stable, and enjoyable for everyone. I have chosen to work with them because I believe they have moved beyond those past issues and are committed to contributing positively to this project. If anyone has any concerns please contact me via DM and I can explain. At the end of the day, we all share the same goal: #SaveATT
